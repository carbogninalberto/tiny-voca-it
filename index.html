02 · Architecture

Small models, sound physics

A FastSpeech-style voice model renders a rough source-filter waveform; a Vocos-style neural vocoder turns its spectrogram into the final audio, starting from the phase of a pulse train at the predicted pitch. One ONNX graph: 28 MB in fp32, 8.6 MB in the int8 version this page loads.

A

Harmonic-prior vocoder

The phase starts from a pulse train built from the predicted pitch, so harmonics stay coherent without a large network. Above 2.5 kHz the pulse share is set per voice to match real speech.

B

Adversarial fine-tuning

Multi-period and multi-resolution critics, a pitch-spread loss against monotone speech, and distillation from Vocos at spectrum level.

C

Two designed voices

Lorenzo and Chiara: designed voices, 5 h of speech each plus emotional speech, screened with Whisper and a voice-similarity check. A 5-frame pitch smoothing removes frame-level tremble.

04 · Notes

Good to know

Limits

  • Italian only. Numbers, prices, abbreviations and foreign names may be read wrongly.
  • Long and short l, n, m, r are not always distinguished.
  • A slight metallic trace remains on some sentences; the emotion control is experimental.
  • No voice cloning and no streaming yet.

Privacy and runtime

  • Everything runs locally with ONNX Runtime Web (single-thread WebAssembly).
  • No text or audio is sent to a server; the model is cached by your browser.
  • Lorenzo and Chiara are synthetic voices, generated with Qwen3-TTS; label their audio as synthetic.
