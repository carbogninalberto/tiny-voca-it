04 · How it was built

Data, alignment and training rounds

1 · Data: real readers and designed voices

The first models learned Italian from CML-TTS Italian (LibriVox audiobook readers, CC BY 4.0): one main reader (IT1, 11.4 h) and five more. The product voices Lorenzo and Chiara are designed voices: Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign created a timbre from a text description, and Qwen3-TTS Base cloned that one reference clip onto ~5 h of sentences each, so the timbre stays fixed. Emotional speech (7 emotions × 2 intensities, 150 emotion-congruent sentences per emotion) was generated the same way.

Every clip is transcribed by Whisper and dropped if the words do not match (WER > 0.1 for training), dropped if spoken too fast or too slow, and emotional clips are dropped if a speaker-verification model (WavLM) says their timbre drifted from the voice's reference. Generation itself was sped up ~3× by replacing Qwen's per-frame sub-code loop with a CUDA graph.

2 · Features and alignment

Each clip becomes: 80-band log-mel frames (hop 256 = 10.7 ms at 24 kHz), the 100-band Vocos log-mel, pitch and voicing per frame, energy, and the phoneme sequence from eSpeak NG. A CTC aligner assigns every phoneme its frames, so the model is trained on supervised durations (no attention-based alignment to learn). Clips whose alignment is weak, whose vowels are unvoiced or whose phones are implausibly long are rejected.

06 · At synthesis time

Small rules that matter

Numbers as words

"€ 25,50" → "venticinque euro e cinquanta", "12:30" → "dodici e trenta", dates, percentages, ordinals (with Italian elisions: ventuno, centotto, ventitré). Identical Python and JavaScript implementations, checked against each other.

Vowels are always voiced

The pitch tracker missed creaky vowels in recordings, so the voicing predictor learned to drop whole vowels (a whispered word). Vowel frames are forced voiced at synthesis.

Pitch smoothing (5 frames)

A Hann low-pass of the predicted log-pitch, with the voiced spread restored, removes frame-level jitter (heard as a tremble) without flattening intonation. Preferred blind on both voices.

Emotion tempo

Emotion is an embedding added to the voice model, scaled by intensity. The model learned the pitch cues but not tempo, so a tiny table adjusts speed: sadness ×0.86, anger ×1.08, surprise ×1.06, fear ×1.05 (linear in intensity).

Streaming

The voice model is non-autoregressive, so the unit of streaming is a sentence: the first sentence plays (after ~0.2–0.8 s) while the next ones are synthesised.

07 · Deployment

One ONNX graph, four precisions

The voice model, the source-filter rendering, the Vocos mel and the vocoder are exported as one ONNX graph that runs in ONNX Runtime and in the browser (ONNX Runtime Web, WebAssembly). The STFT tables (≈20 MB) are computed inside the graph from the windows, so the full-precision file is 28 MB. Quantized versions keep the signal-processing exact and only compress the learned weights.
