tiny-voca italiano

A 4.5M-parameter Italian text-to-speech model trained from scratch. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere. First visit downloads about 40 MB, then it is cached. Code and models on GitHub.

About the voices

  • IT1: the Italian base model, trained for 100k steps on 11.4 hours of one CML-TTS reader. On held-out test sentences Whisper transcribes it with 7.1% word errors (the original recordings: 9.1%).
  • IT2: the same model adapted to a different reader with only 28 minutes of audio, the custom-voice fine-tuning demo.

Known limits

  • Long and short l, n, m, r are not always distinguished (pala may sound like palla).
  • The browser uses eSpeak NG 1.52 (WebAssembly); the model was trained with 1.51 tokens. They agree on the vast majority of the training texts.
  • Names and foreign words can be read wrongly; one sentence at a time works best.
