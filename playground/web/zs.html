Zero-shot voices

A pilot of the zero-shot model: the voice comes from a fingerprint of a short clip instead of a trained voice slot, so it can speak in voices it never saw during training. Everything runs in your browser; your recording never leaves your device.

Pilot quality, trained for a few hours on 180 synthetic voices: expect the general timbre (gender, pitch, brightness), not a convincing copy. Only use your own voice or voices you have permission to use.
